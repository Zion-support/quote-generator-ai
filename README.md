## What it does

Quote Generator AI builds accurate, professional quotes from CRM deal context and your price book — with discount guardrails enforced and approval routing built in.

## Key features

- **One-click quotes** — line items pulled from deal context and price book.
- **Guardrail enforcement** — out-of-band pricing auto-routes for approval.
- **Multi-currency & tax** — regional pricing, tax, and legal footers handled.
- **Branded templates** — PDFs and web quotes that match your brand.
- **Quote analytics** — open rates, time-to-sign, and win rate per template.

## Who it's for

Sales teams losing deals to slow or sloppy quoting.
